05 / Graphic language

Paths with purpose.

Angular folds and directional planes guide composition toward important information. They should imply focus and progress, never become decorative clutter.

One journey. Many ways through it.

Use thin, low-contrast paths behind large messages, release artwork, onboarding, and installer moments.

Purposeful geometry

Controls use 6 px corners. Panels and repeated items use no more than 8 px. Avoid pill shapes unless the control is genuinely compact.

Color follows function

Cyan is action, cobalt is depth, teal is positive movement. Coral, amber, and green communicate status only.

07 / Usage

Keep the identity recognizable.

Consistency makes the new mark feel established. These rules apply to the browser, website, installer, social graphics, documentation, and videos.

Logo

  • Use approved SVG masters whenever possible.
  • Maintain one-quarter-symbol clear space.
  • Use monochrome white when color reproduction is limited.

Avoid

  • Do not rotate, stretch, bevel, glow, or outline the logo.
  • Keep the cyan O segment exactly as supplied in the approved master.
  • Do not separate, rearrange, or recolor the ribbon facets.
  • Do not place the mark on visually noisy imagery.

Naming

  • Use Minova in user-facing product language.
  • Use Minova Chromium in technical and release contexts.
  • Keep spelling and capitalization consistent.

08 / Voice

Direct, capable, and on the user's side.

Minova speaks like a knowledgeable collaborator. It explains what matters, respects the person's time, and never inflates simple features into grand claims.

Product copy

“Your update is ready. Restart Minova when you are ready to install it.”

State what happened, what comes next, and whether the user is in control.

Marketing copy

“A browser that makes room for the way you work.”

Lead with tangible value. Keep headlines concise and support them with specific capabilities.
