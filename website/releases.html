Minova Chromium 1.0.5

Version 1.0.5 adds the Safari-style interface, Liquid Glass, and Minova's complete new visual identity.

Interface and identity

  • Safari UI: Centered Smart Search, traffic-light controls, integrated tools, and rounded tabs below the toolbar.
  • Liquid Glass: Layered translucent chrome with practical contrast and a restrained light appearance.
  • Prism M: A consistent symbol and custom wordmark across the executable, browser, installer, website, and release artwork.
  • Brand kit: Vector masters, platform exports, tokens, templates, and usage guidance in one package.

1.0.3 July 26, 2026

Minova Chromium 1.0.3

Version 1.0.3 turns Minova into a browser that can adapt to both traditional browsing and larger multi-context workflows.

New interfaces and workflows

  • Workspace UI: A collapsible vertical-tab sidebar with color-coded contexts and live site icons.
  • Classic UI: A complete horizontal-tab option for people who prefer a traditional browser layout.
  • Custom workspaces: Create, rename, recolor, reorder, and remove contexts without losing their tabs.
  • Split View: Tile and interact with two regular webpages inside one Minova window.
  • First-launch tour: Choose a layout visually, learn the major browser tools, and finish with secure Google password import.

Streaming and reliability

  • Streaming Mode now coordinates with both interface layouts, collapses the workspace rail, and blocks incompatible Split View entry.
  • The attached protected-playback surface follows window movement, resizing, minimize and restore, and fullscreen transitions more reliably.
  • Streaming playback can remain paused in the background while another tab is active, and closes with its owning tab.
  • Expanded automated coverage for onboarding, workspace editing, tiled views, Classic UI, and streaming lifecycle behavior.
  • A versioned GPL-3.0 application-source archive now ships alongside the installer and updater assets.
